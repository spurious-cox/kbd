## Description

KBD is a number pad that floats above everything else on your Mac and types
into whichever text field has your cursor — in any application. Spreadsheets,
scoring software, web forms, databases, your own tools: if it accepts typing,
it accepts KBD.

**It never takes your place.** The keypad is built as a non-activating panel,
so pressing a key does not make KBD the active app and does not move the
cursor out of the field you're filling in. Your text field stays exactly as
you left it — cursor blinking, selection intact — and the digit simply
arrives. This is the whole point of KBD, and every design decision serves it.

**It types for real.** Keys send genuine keyboard events, the same kind a
physical keyboard sends, rather than pasting or scripting. Applications can't
tell the difference, so there is nothing to configure and no per-app support
list. Backspace corrects, the decimal point does what it should.

**It goes where you want it.** Drag from anywhere on the coloured field to
move the pad — the whole background is the handle, not a thin title bar. Park
it beside the column you're filling in and it stays there, above your windows,
on every Space, including full-screen apps.

**It's the size you need it.** Drag the corner grip, option-drag the field, or
choose a size from the right-click menu — 75% up to 200%, or anywhere in
between. The entire keypad scales as one piece, so the proportions stay right
whether you want it small and out of the way or large enough to hit without
looking.

**It's the colour you want.** Right-click anywhere for eight field colours,
from bright blue to charcoal. Pick the one that reads best against whatever
you're working in; the labels adjust automatically to stay legible.

**It stays until you're done.** KBD keeps working across app switches for as
long as you need it, and closes when you press DISMISS — not before. Your
position, size and colour come back the way you left them next time you open
it.

## Feature list

- Types into the focused text field in any application
- Never steals focus — your cursor and selection stay put
- Sends real keyboard events; no per-app setup or compatibility list
- Digits, decimal point and backspace
- Drag from anywhere on the pad to reposition
- Floats above all windows, on every Space and over full-screen apps
- Resizable from 60% to 300%, scaling as a single unit
- Eight field colours, with labels that stay readable on each
- Remembers position, size and colour between launches
- Stays open until dismissed

## Requirements

macOS 13 or later. KBD needs Accessibility permission (System Settings >
Privacy & Security > Accessibility) in order to send keystrokes to other
applications — macOS will ask the first time you open it.
